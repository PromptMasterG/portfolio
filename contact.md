## Ways to work with me {#ways}

These are the three kinds of work the projects on this site prepare me for. I scope each one with you before starting.

- **An evaluation suite for an AI feature.** A set of real and adversarial cases for a model-backed feature, run, with a report of what passes, what fails and what I would fix first. The model for this is the persona suite in [Samara]({{ '/work/samara/#verification' | relative_url }}).
- **An agent or MCP server around a workflow.** A small, deliberately limited tool catalogue, spend ceilings that stop a run, and an audit trail. The model for this is the [AI Studio Tool]({{ '/work/ai-studio-tool/#agents' | relative_url }}).
- **An AI video series pipeline.** Records for the cast and places, a knowledge lookup that the agent playbooks require before generating, and a repeatable prompt structure. The model for this is the desktop app in the AI Studio Tool, used on [a real series]({{ '/work/ai-studio-tool/#example' | relative_url }}).
