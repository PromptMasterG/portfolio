I am a medical doctor by training (MBChB, University of Cape Town, 2015 to 2021). Since 2025 I have been the founder and content director of Mafia Cat Studio and Prompt Master G, which is when I moved into AI products and AI video. I am based in Johannesburg, South Africa.

## What I make

I build local and agentic AI systems and I make AI video. The work on this site is a local AI journal, an MCP server with ten specialist agents, a separate desktop app that holds the records and prompts for a real series, a video-grading app and a free public prompt tool. For video I use Veo 3, CapCut Pro and ElevenLabs.

Before AI I made live-action content for a biosciences company, across LinkedIn, TikTok and YouTube. [Content before AI]({{ '/content/#earlier' | relative_url }}) has the results.

## How I build

I build with Claude Code. I write the specification, set the acceptance gates, review the results and decide what ships. The code is written by AI coding agents under those specifications, and I verify it by running the software, reading the test results and checking its claims against sources. That is the skill I am offering: specifying, directing, evaluating and verifying AI-built systems.

I run models on my own GPU, I measure whether something works before I say it does, and I publish my corrections. The [How I build]({{ '/how-i-build/' | relative_url }}) page shows each habit with a real example.

- **AI engineer or AI developer:** applied AI, agents, MCP, local model inference, retrieval and evaluation.
- **AI content creator or AI video producer:** series production, Veo 3 workflows, YouTube analytics and content operations.
